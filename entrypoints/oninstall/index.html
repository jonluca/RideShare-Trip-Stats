Quick start

Three steps. About a minute.

Collect rides from Uber Trips or orders from Uber Eats, then optionally import your official archive as a fallback.

  1. 01

    Open a history page

    Open Uber Trips for rides or Uber Eats Past Orders for deliveries, sign in, and make sure recent history appears.

  2. 02

    Find the matching launcher

    A draggable Analyze trips or Analyze Eats button appears automatically. If the page was already open during installation, refresh it once.

  3. 03

    Analyze and import

    Move the button wherever you like, then click it. You can also use the extension’s toolbar icon. Leave the tab open while analysis runs. On the dashboard, choose Import Uber data to merge an official ZIP as an archive fallback.

⌁

Privacy by design

Your data never leaves your browser.

The extension reads rides or Eats orders only when you start it while signed in, and parses only the Uber ZIP/CSV files you select. Both dashboards are calculated locally and stored in local extension storage. It does not use an analytics server.

Having trouble?

Three quick checks

  • Make sure recent rides or orders are visible on the matching Uber history page.
  • If the launcher does not appear after installation or an update, refresh the Uber tab once.
  • Use Import Uber data with your official ZIP to fill gaps or add older history.
