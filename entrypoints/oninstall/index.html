Quick start

Three steps. About a minute.

Collect rides from Uber Trips, then optionally import your official Uber archive for Eats and older ride history.

  1. 01

    Sign in to Uber

    Open riders.uber.com/trips and make sure your past trips appear.

  2. 02

    Find the trip launcher

    A draggable Analyze trips button appears automatically on Uber. If the page was already open during installation, refresh it once.

  3. 03

    Analyze and import

    Move the button wherever you like, then click it. You can also use the extension’s toolbar icon. Leave the tab open while analysis runs. On the dashboard, choose Import Uber data to add Eats orders or older rides from your official ZIP.

⌁

Privacy by design

Your data never leaves your browser.

The extension reads rides only while you are signed in and parses only the Uber ZIP/CSV files you select. Both dashboards are calculated locally and stored in local extension storage. It does not use an analytics server.

Having trouble?

Two quick checks

  • Make sure past trips are visible at riders.uber.com/trips.
  • If the launcher does not appear after installation or an update, refresh the Uber tab once.
  • Use Import Uber data with your official ZIP to add Uber Eats and older ride history.
